UNDERSTAND YOUR CYCLE

Four phases. One unique journey.

Menstrual cycles can be described through four commonly discussed phases.

♡

Menstrual

The period phase, when menstrual bleeding occurs.

✦

Follicular

A phase associated with follicle development and changing hormones.

◌

Ovulation

The phase around the release of an egg from an ovary.

❀

Luteal

The phase after ovulation and before the next menstrual period.

Important: Cycle information shown by HerGlow is an educational estimate and may not reflect your individual cycle. It is not intended for contraception, diagnosis, or medical decisions.
